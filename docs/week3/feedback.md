WEEK 3 Design review

Screen 1 (Get ticket / customer) FR-01

Works well :
Phone, 3 buttons in 1 column - big touch buttons. The text with instruction above
buttons. It is ok to use thumb on mobile, holding the phone in one hand

Problem:
All buttons are named "TEXT/BUTTON", looks the same important, so the customer cannot understand which button will give number for me. Not pass 5sec test. Not match real world. Final should be like use in real world situations. Our users say for example "Get my number", not "Submit", "Join"

Change:
Real labels for all buttons


Screen 2 (my place / waiting) - FR-02/03

Works well:
Ticket number, ticket status. On the top of phone frame. Above of queue list. First sight for person, without scroll

Problem:
small pills with number and status. So the biggest element. The most important thing to a person waiting in line is position in line. This is not represented at all. In the pop-up for FR-03, the alert is over the ticket no and status. This breaks status visibility. Because it hides the person's status when it matters the most. "its your turn". 

Change:
Make the ticket number, position (3 ppl ahead) the biggest element. Make that the main thing on the page.downsize the full list of queue and make that below it or "people ahead"Make the pop up as a banner up top. Leave the number visible.Add a dismiss button and clear message. "Your turn. go to window 3"Add a short instruction line to the phone frame.

Screen 3. Staff Dashboard. - FR-04 / FR-05 / FR-06

Works well:
On the phone. Call next is darker than other elements. So it seems as the main action. On laptop, show, No-show. Big separate buttons. Not easy to miss when busy counter.

Problem:
In one round of work, (see queue, call next, show or no show) one round of work.split to 3 separate views. So in order to do what he/she does most often, needs to switch screens. The person you are looking for is not visible when looking at the queue (violation of visibility of status). The Show/No-show is right next to each other and has equal importance without a confirm or undo. So one mis-tap on a busy counter. Eliminates the person from the queue. (error prevention) The word "Show" is a little vague. People would be more likely to say "Served" Also, minor things are: On the phone, Call Next overlaps the box for Up Next. The size of Show/No-show is different. Inconsistent.


Change:
Combine into one dash board. Put current person at top. The largest button would be Call Next. Followed by Up next and then the list for queue.Rename "Show" to "Served". Separate out the Served and no show. Short "Undo" option after no-show.Fix the overlap. Same size for both buttons.
